# Design System: ArgusOs (Cyber Neon Gaming Hub)

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Neon Hacker Arabic Gaming Hub"**

ArgusOs is the premier Arabic game-localization portal designed specifically for modern gamers.
Visual world: Deep obsidian black, glowing emerald/neon green accents, high-contrast holographic HUD panels, and dynamic cyber elements.
Brand mark: The Hooded Cyber Hacker with luminous `</>` visor and targeting reticles.

## Colors & Surfaces

- **Background:** Deep obsidian `#020503` with subtle radial scanlines and dark vignettes.
- **Surface:** `#07100a` / `#0d1e12` with cyber hairline borders (`#132d19`).
- **Accent:** Electric Neon Green `#00ff66`, used for high-impact actions, callouts, active indicators, and glowing highlights.
- **Text:** Crisp cyber-white `#e6fcf0` on dark grounds for maximum legibility and zero eye fatigue.
